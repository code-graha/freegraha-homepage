How it works

How to get a freegraha.in subdomain

A freegraha.in subdomain comes free with projects WebGraha designs and builds. Here's how a project gets here.

  1. 01

    Work with WebGraha

    Start a project with us: a website, web app, portfolio or landing page.

  2. 02

    We design & build

    Our team crafts your project with the same care as every WebGraha build.

  3. 03

    Pick your subdomain

    Choose a name and we set up yourproject.freegraha.in for you, DNS and HTTPS included.

  4. 04

    Launch

    Your project goes live and joins the freegraha.in directory.

Eligibility

Who gets a freegraha.in subdomain?

freegraha.in is reserved for projects WebGraha has designed or built. It isn't open for public sign-ups.

Eligible

  • Websites designed or developed by WebGraha
  • Web apps and landing pages built by WebGraha
  • Portfolios and personal sites made by WebGraha
  • Staging or preview builds of active WebGraha projects

Not eligible

  • Sites built by you or another agency
  • Projects no longer maintained with WebGraha
  • Content that's illegal, misleading or harmful
  • Reselling or sub-leasing a subdomain

FAQ

Questions about freegraha.in

What is freegraha.in?

freegraha.in is a domain run by WebGraha, a boutique web design and development studio. Every website, web app or portfolio that WebGraha designs and builds can get a free subdomain on it, such as mohitsingh.freegraha.in, set up and managed by WebGraha with HTTPS.

Who can get a freegraha.in subdomain?

Only projects designed or built by WebGraha. It's a perk for WebGraha clients, not a public sign-up service.

I built my own site. Can I get one?

Not at the moment. If you'd like WebGraha to build or redesign your site, start a project at webgraha.com/contact and a freegraha.in subdomain comes with it.

How do I get a freegraha.in subdomain?

Start a project with WebGraha at webgraha.com/contact. Once WebGraha has designed and built your website or app, you pick a name and WebGraha sets up yourproject.freegraha.in for you, including DNS and HTTPS.

Does it cost anything extra?

No. The subdomain, its DNS setup and HTTPS come free with your WebGraha project.

What exactly do I get?

An address like yourproject.freegraha.in, set up and managed by WebGraha, served over HTTPS, plus a listing in the freegraha.in project directory.

Can I move to my own domain later?

Yes. WebGraha can register and connect a custom domain for your project whenever you're ready.
